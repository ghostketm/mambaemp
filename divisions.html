Group Architecture & Profiles

Six Disciplined
Business Divisions

Day-one legal structure: a single Kenyan private limited company, Mamba Empire Ltd, registered through the Business Registration Service (BRS). Divisions share legal, accounting, compliance, and IP, but maintain separate books with discrete profit targets. A division is spun out into its own subsidiary only when triggers are met ($250K+ revenue, regulatory mandate, or outside equity).

The Commercial Offering

High-grade traceable specialty coffee, single-origin highland tea, and verified organic honey purchased directly from farmer cooperatives and aggregators under binding forward contracts, then exported to wholesale roasters and global merchants.

Planned Year-1 Portfolio Mix: Specialty Coffee (~50%), Organic Raw Honey (~25%), Highland Orthodox Tea (~25%).

Trading Discipline & Reality Check

Commodity trading is a thin-margin business. We benchmark realistically at 18% gross and 6% net, using certified micro-lots to boost realization. Purchases are financed strictly through 30% buyer deposits or Letters of Credit (LCs), keeping stock inventory days strictly controlled.

Compliance: AFA license, KEPHIS phytosanitary clearance, and full compliance with the European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR).

Core Services & SaaS Products

ZenIT designs enterprise software, AI-driven process automation, cloud infrastructure, and an end-to-end farm-to-buyer traceability platform. Built originally to power KOSA's supply chain, it is commercialized to third-party agri-exporters and trading firms.

Revenue Model: Initial project fees + ongoing monthly engineering retainers and recurring SaaS licensing. Target: 40% recurring revenue by Year 2.

Payments & Banking Architecture

Direct API integrations with licensed payment service providers (M-PESA Daraja, bank rails, and card gateways). ZenIT deliberately avoids holding or pooling client funds in Year 1, completely circumventing Central Bank payment system licensing requirements.

Security Offerings

Holding & Shared Services Group Governance & IP Custodian

Mamba Empire (Core)

Centralized Administration, Compliance, Treasury & Capital Allocation

Core functions as the group's holding and custodian unit. In Year 1, it operates as a shared cost centre absorbing $14,000 in unified accounting, legal counsel, insurance, corporate banking, and governance expenses across all divisions.

Subsidiary Spin-Out Triggers

Divisions remain operational units within Mamba Empire Ltd until specific milestones are reached:

  • Annual division revenue exceeds $250,000.
  • A regulatory authority specifically mandates a dedicated licensed entity.
  • Third-party equity or strategic investment is accepted for a specific vertical.
  • Operational liability isolation is required (e.g., external security audit underwriting).
