## 3. THE 3-COLOR RULE (STRICT)

Use exactly three brand colors in a 60 / 30 / 10 ratio. Two harmonize as the base. One contrasts and is reserved for action.

| Role | Share | Color | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dominant (base) | 60% | Obsidian | `#0A0A0A` | Page backgrounds, nav, footer |
| Secondary (harmony) | 30% | Deep Emerald | `#043927` | Alternate sections, cards, panels, gradients with Obsidian |
| **Accent (contrast)** | **10%** | **Metallic Gold** | **`#D4AF37`** | **Buttons, CTAs, links on hover, key figures, active states, focus rings, important highlights only** |

**Neutral text colors (not counted as brand colors):**
- Primary text: Ivory `#F5F5F0`
- Secondary text: Ivory at 70% opacity
- Borders and dividers: Ivory at 10–15% opacity

**Rules**
1. Gold is the *only* color that signals "click here" or "this matters." Never use gold for decoration, large backgrounds, or body text.
2. Do not add any other hue. Derive variations only through opacity, or a 5–10% lightness shift of the same three colors.
3. Gold buttons use Obsidian text (contrast above 9:1). Hover state is a slightly brighter gold plus a subtle lift, not a new color.
4. Secondary buttons: transparent with a 1px gold outline and ivory text.
5. Define all colors as CSS variables/design tokens in one file, for example `--color-base`, `--color-surface`, `--color-accent`, `--color-text`. No hard-coded hex anywhere else.
6. All text must meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text).

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY, SPACING & LAYOUT SYSTEM

**Typography (max 2 families, self-hosted or `font-display: swap`, preloaded)**
- Display/institutional statements: refined serif (e.g. Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display).
- Navigation, body, UI, labels: clean sans (e.g. Inter or Manrope).
- Fluid type scale using `clamp()`: H1 56–96px, H2 36–56px, H3 24–32px, body 16–18px, label/eyebrow 12–13px uppercase with 0.12em tracking.
- Body line-height 1.6–1.7, headings 1.1–1.2, max line length 65–75 characters.

**Spacing (8-point system)**
- Tokens: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 160px.
- Section vertical padding: 96px mobile, 128px tablet, 160px desktop.
- Gap between heading and content: 48px. Gap between cards: 24px.

**Grid**
- 12-column grid, max content width 1280px, wide-bleed sections allowed.
- Side gutters: 20px mobile, 40px tablet, 64px desktop.
- Alternate section backgrounds (Obsidian → Emerald-tinted → Obsidian) to create rhythm.
- Every section follows a consistent structure: **eyebrow label → headline → one short paragraph (max 40 words) → visual/interactive content → single CTA.**

**Visual treatment**
- Subtle glassmorphism only on cards (backdrop blur, 5–8% ivory fill, 1px ivory-10% border).
- Fine gold hairline dividers, used sparingly.
- Consistent corner radius (suggest 12px cards, 999px pills for buttons).

## 8. KEY PAGE REQUIREMENTS

**`/empire`:** interactive map of parent → subsidiaries → investments → partnerships → future ventures. Filter chips: *All, Technology, Consulting, Digital Services, Infrastructure, Investments, Future Ventures*. Keyboard-navigable, with a list view fallback on mobile.

**`/governance`:** clean, scannable accordion/tab layout: Framework, Ethics, Risk, Compliance, Policies, Accountability. Secondary visual weight.

**`/contact`:** tabs for *Investor Relations, Business Partnerships, Media, Careers, General*. Real forms with validation, honeypot spam protection, inline errors, success/failure states, and a per-tab email routing config.

- Every entity carries a status: `operating | established | strategic-investment | in-development | planned | partnership`. Show it as a badge. Never present a planned venture as operating.
- Missing information becomes a clean, content-ready placeholder structure flagged in a `CONTENT_TODO.md`, never invented copy.
- Maximum 40 words per paragraph on landing sections. Use headings, labels and visuals instead of prose.

## 11. MOTION & INTERACTION

- Scroll-triggered fade/translate reveals (IntersectionObserver), subtle parallax on hero only.
- Motion tokens: 200ms micro, 400–600ms reveals, easing `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.
- Animate only `transform` and `opacity`.
- Smooth page transitions, animated nav underline, hover-responsive cards and ecosystem nodes.
- **Must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`** (disable parallax and reveals, keep content visible).
- No autoplaying sound, no layout shift from animation.

- Custom mobile navigation: full-screen overlay menu, large touch targets (min 44×44px), sticky gold "Contact" action.
- Re-compose layouts for mobile (stack, reorder, simplify the ecosystem map). Do not just shrink desktop.

## 13. SEO (TECHNICAL AND CONTENT)

- Semantic HTML5 landmarks (`header, nav, main, section, article, footer`). **Exactly one H1 per page**, with logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- Unique `<title>` (≤60 chars) and meta description (≤155 chars) per page. Title template: `Page Name | Mamba Empire`.
- Canonical URL, Open Graph and Twitter Card tags, and a 1200×630 OG image for each key page.
- JSON-LD structured data: `Organization` (with logo, sameAs, contactPoint) site-wide, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList` on inner pages, `Article` on news, `Person` on leadership where verified.
- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `manifest.webmanifest`, descriptive URL slugs, breadcrumbs, and internal linking between related companies, sectors and news.
- Images: descriptive alt text, WebP/AVIF, explicit width/height, `loading="lazy"` below the fold, responsive `srcset`.
- Custom 404 with helpful links. Proper 301 handling for any old routes.
- **Performance targets:** LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, INP < 200ms, Lighthouse ≥ 95 (Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, SEO). Preload hero font and critical CSS, code-split by route, no heavy libraries for simple effects.
