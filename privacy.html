Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Mamba Empire Ltd complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and registration requirements of the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

1. Data Controller

Mamba Empire Ltd, incorporated in the Republic of Kenya, acts as the primary data controller for personal and commercial data collected via this digital headquarters and its operating divisions.

2. Data We Process

We process commercial contact information provided directly by institutional partners, prospective clients, and applicants. We do not sell, rent, or lease personal data to third parties.

3. Security & Cryptographic Protection

All data stored by Mamba Empire Core and ZenIT platforms is encrypted using industry-standard protocols, protected by role-based access restrictions, and audited regularly by independent security practitioners.

4. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For inquiries regarding your personal data rights, write to our compliance desk at privacy@mambaempire.com.
