Institutional Integrity

Governance, Risk & Compliance

Trust is our core product. We enforce strict statutory adherence with Kenyan and international regulatory authorities, mandate arm's-length interdivisional governance, and proactively hedge downside risks.

Mamba Empire Ltd is incorporated under the Business Registration Service (BRS) in the Republic of Kenya. All intellectual property, trademarks, and brand assets are held centrally by Mamba Empire Core. Business divisions operate under discrete ledgers with dedicated accounting transparency.
We maintain full compliance with Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) corporate tax regulations and electronic tax invoice management systems (eTIMS). Cross-divisional workflows comply with arm's-length transfer pricing requirements.
Registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) pursuant to the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Strict encryption, role-based access control, and privacy protocols govern all digital workflows across ZenIT, BanditSec, and our education platform.
To preserve absolute credibility, BanditSec operates under a permanent governance covenant prohibiting it from auditing or certifying software built by ZenIT. External third-party auditing firms underwrite Mamba Empire's own infrastructure.
Trade price volatility is hedged using forward fixed-price contracts. Counterparty buyer default is mitigated by mandatory 30% upfront deposits and letters of credit. Client revenue concentration is strictly capped so no single client represents over 30% of any division’s revenue.
