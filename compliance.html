Board & Advisory Panel

At least three seated members, including dedicated representatives with extensive corporate finance experience and agricultural export compliance. Meets quarterly to audit gates.

Profit Retention Rule

At least 50% of net earnings are retained in the ecosystem through Year 2 and redeployed into the highest-return divisions. Distributions only commence after maintaining a six-month fixed reserve.

Related-Party Covenant

All transactions between divisions or with founders are formally documented in writing, priced strictly at arm's length (transfer pricing rules), and ratified by board resolution.

Appendix · Strategic Evolution

What Was Refined and Why

How the 2026 Master Plan evolved from earlier conceptual notes into a defensible, bankable operational framework.

Original Concept Refined 2026 Plan Underlying Rationale
Figures in Kenya Shillings All figures in USD Easier for global trade partners and institutional investors; pegged at ~KSh 129.7 / USD.
Combined gross margin shown as 53.1% 36.0% blended gross margin Recomputed accurately from individual division revenue weightings (previously did not reconcile).
40% service / 18% commodity net margins 10%–18% services, 6% KOSA First-year operating ventures rarely achieve 40% net; commodity trade is fundamentally thin-margin.
Holding valued separately ($38M–$63M) Core not valued separately Avoids double-counting; holding value equals the sum of the underlying operating divisions.
3x to 5x net profit valuation Revenue multiples (0.3x–2.0x) Early-stage net profit is small and volatile; revenue multiples offer standard benchmarking.
No group overhead budgeted $14,000 shared costs added Reflects real corporate expenses for accounting, legal, audit, insurance, and compliance.
No funding requirement specified $74,800 launch capital budget Clear capital deployment ($40K trade revolving capital, $6K legal/licensing, $8K tech stack).
Six separate incorporated 'groups' One company, six divisions Lower corporate tax complexity and administrative overhead; spin out only upon meeting milestones.
