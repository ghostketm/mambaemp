Framework 6.1

Year-1 Base Case Divisional Breakdown

Division net profit is stated before group shared costs ($14,000), covering accounting, tax, legal, company secretarial, insurance, compliance, banking, and governance.

Key Year-1 Operating Assumptions:

  • Revenue ramp: ~70% of annual revenue falls in H2 because regulatory approvals, licensing, and trade forward contracts take time to finalize.
  • KOSA terms: 18% gross margin after freight, packaging, export phytosanitary documentation, and grading. Financed via 30% buyer deposit and balance against bills of lading.
  • ZenIT model: 60% gross margin delivered by small core leadership plus specialized vetted contractors. Cloud and server costs accounted for.
  • BanditSec model: 65% gross margin after vulnerability scanning tooling, licensing, and testing specialists.
Framework 6.3

Three-Year Growth Outlook (Base Case)

Margins expand from 6.0% to 12.0% as recurring software & security retainers compound, KOSA bulk trading lowers unit shipping overhead, and central shared costs scale across higher volume.
