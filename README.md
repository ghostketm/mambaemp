# Mamba Empire Ltd — Corporate & Ecosystem Website

**Refined Master Plan | 2026 Edition**  
Operational Blueprint, Group Structure & Financial Framework (Kenya)

---

## Overview
This static corporate website represents the complete operational, divisional, and financial blueprint for **Mamba Empire Ltd**, directly matching the refined master plan in `mamba.pdf`.

### Architecture & Design
- **Single Legal Entity with Six Disciplined Divisions:** KOSA (Agri-Trade), ZenIT (Tech & AI Automation), BanditSec (Cybersecurity & Digital Trust), Portfolio & Education (Bootcamps), IKR (Infrastructure & Warehousing), and Core (Holding & Shared Services).
- **Modern Responsive Design:** Crafted in deep obsidian, forest emerald (`#0f3d2e`), and warm gold (`#c5a358`), with Fraunces serif typography and clean tabular financial modeling.
- **Pure Static Stack:** Zero build step or runtime dependencies. Simply double-click `index.html` or upload to any static hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, GitHub Pages, Apache/Nginx).

---

## Page Structure
1. **[index.html](index.html):** Executive summary, interactive ecosystem flywheel diagram, divisional overviews, 5 core operating principles, stage-gate snapshot, and partnership contact form.
2. **[divisions.html](divisions.html):** In-depth divisional profiles, Year-1 revenue & gross/net margin benchmarks, commercial models, EUDR/AFA compliance, and subsidiary spin-out triggers.
3. **[financials.html](financials.html):** Complete financial plan including Year-1 base case ($349,000 revenue / $21,045 net profit), 3-year growth outlook ($1.48M target), Year-1 scenarios, $74,800 launch capital budget breakdown, and indicative revenue multiples.
4. **[roadmap.html](roadmap.html):** 36-month stage-gated implementation roadmap detailing concrete gates for Foundation (Months 0–3), Activation (Months 3–9), Expansion (Months 9–18), and Scale (Months 18–36).
5. **[compliance.html](compliance.html):** Kenya regulatory checklist (BRS, KRA eTIMS, ODPC Data Protection, AFA, CBK NPS framework), comprehensive risk mitigation matrix, monthly divisional KPIs, board governance covenants, and historical refinement appendix.

## Data Management
All business metrics, divisional targets, regulatory entries, and financial tables are dynamically bound from `assets/js/data.js` and rendered via `assets/js/app.js`.
